# 06-timeline - Jeremy Clarkson

## Timeline

### 1960

- Born 11 April 1960 in Doncaster, South Yorkshire, England.
- Biographical summary corroborated by widely available references, including public encyclopedia summaries and broadcaster profiles.

### Early career

- Worked in local journalism before establishing himself as a motoring writer.
- In GQ 2020 he recalls starting on local papers and learning that car journalism could get him loan cars and better stories.

### Top Gear era

- Became strongly associated with Top Gear and helped define its brash, entertainment-led modern identity.
- Public references consistently describe the Clarkson-led version as a global breakthrough for the format.

### 2015 rupture

- Left the BBC-era Top Gear after the incident that led to the non-renewal of his contract.
- Public reporting and official BBC materials make this a major career rupture.

### 2016 onward

- Re-emerged with The Grand Tour on Amazon alongside Richard Hammond, James May, and producer Andy Wilman.
- GQ 2016 frames the new show as a close cousin rather than a radical reinvention.

### 2021 onward

- Clarkson's Farm launches on Prime Video.
- Prime Video's own summary describes it as an intense and frequently hilarious year in the life of Britain's most unlikely farmer.
- BBC 2021 and Guardian 2024 suggest this phase changed his public image more than expected.

### 2024

- Guardian profile presents him as more affected by farming economics, planning fights, and climate variation than his older public caricature would imply.

### 2025

- Top Gear interview shows continued preference for older, more tactile cars and continued hostility to touchscreen-heavy interfaces.

## Timeline-based observations

- The through-line is not "cars" or "farming" but performative judgment.
- He repeatedly moves from one institution/platform to another while keeping the same role:
  - find friction
  - dramatize it
  - make it funny
  - turn it into mass communication
