# 05-decisions - Jeremy Clarkson

## Key decisions / inflection points

### 1. Build a career around writing first, motoring second

Evidence:
- GQ 2020: he says he only got into cars because he enjoyed writing.

Interpretation:
- Cars were the vehicle, not the original calling.
- This helps explain why he reviews through narrative, embarrassment, freedom, and desirability rather than pure mechanics.

### 2. Rebuild rather than retire after the BBC break

Evidence:
- After the 2015 BBC split, he, Hammond, May, and Wilman quickly regrouped and created The Grand Tour for Amazon. GQ 2016 frames the move as reinvention without disowning the old formula.

Interpretation:
- He preserves the entertainment core while changing the platform.
- This suggests a strong bias toward continuity of audience relationship over purity about institution.

### 3. Turn the farm from passive asset into active operating life

Evidence:
- BBC 2021 and Farmers Guide 2021 both note he bought the farm in 2008 but only took it over directly after the manager retired.
- He says he thought he could do it; instead he found heartbreak, low margins, and constant setbacks.

Interpretation:
- Clarkson often enters domains by underestimating them.
- But once in, he tends to publicize the friction rather than hide it.

### 4. Shift from climate-mocking posture to climate-affected realism

Evidence:
- BBC 2021 acknowledges the irony that weather volatility was hitting him directly.
- Guardian 2024 has him saying earlier dismissal was part of the caricature and that farming made climate variability impossible to ignore.

Interpretation:
- He does revise, but usually only after consequences are concrete.
- His worldview is materially revisionist, not morally convert-like.

### 5. Preference for older, simpler machines over menu-driven complexity

Evidence:
- Top Gear 2025 interview: he explains why he keeps older cars with physical buttons and finds new-car interface complexity maddening.

Interpretation:
- Core principle: if a product makes ordinary control harder, sophistication is failure.

## Decision heuristics emerging

- Trust friction over theory.
- If a thing cannot be explained plainly, it is probably being oversold.
- If a system requires too many menus, committees, or permissions, it is probably broken.
- Preserve what gives pleasure and legibility; attack what inserts mediation and pretense.
