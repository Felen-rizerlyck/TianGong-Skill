# 06-timeline - Richard Hammond

## Timeline

### 1969

- Born December 19, 1969.

### Journalism and presenting

- Established himself as a presenter and motoring personality before global Top Gear fame.

### Top Gear era

- Became one of the defining faces of the revived Top Gear beginning in 2002.

### 2006 crash

- The Vampire dragster crash and coma became the central biographical rupture in his public story.

### 2016 onward

- Continued with Clarkson and May on The Grand Tour after the BBC split.

### DriveTribe / workshop era

- Co-founded DriveTribe with Clarkson and May.
- Developed Richard Hammond's Workshop / The Smallest Cog identity around restoration and classic-car stewardship.

### 2025

- Public comments around electrification emphasize continuity of enthusiasm rather than resistance to change.

## Timeline-based observations

- The through-line is not just speed, but attachment.
- Hammond's career arc moves from thrill-seeking display toward affectionate custodianship.
- He remains the most openly sentimental member of the trio's motoring worldview.
