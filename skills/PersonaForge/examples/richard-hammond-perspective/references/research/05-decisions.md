# 05-decisions - Richard Hammond

## Key decisions / inflection points

### 1. Build a career on enthusiasm, not aloof authority

Evidence:
- Across interviews and presenting work, Hammond consistently succeeds by making viewers feel invited rather than lectured.

Interpretation:
- His authority comes from emotional access and commitment, not from detached mastery.

### 2. Continue high-performance motoring identity after major trauma

Evidence:
- Post-2006 crash reflections in public interviews center on family and perspective, but do not reject the machine world that nearly killed him.

Interpretation:
- Hammond's model is not "eliminate risk". It is "risk and love coexist, but consequences are real."

### 3. Expand from speed spectacle into restoration and stewardship

Evidence:
- Workshop-era public materials focus on classic restoration, business-building, and preserving beloved machinery.

Interpretation:
- He matures from pure excitement to custodianship without abandoning excitement.

### 4. Defend emotional continuity through technological transition

Evidence:
- The 2025 electrification comments argue that the shift in propulsion should not mean the death of passion.

Interpretation:
- He is technology-flexible but emotion-rigid: the machine can change, but love must remain.

## Decision heuristics emerging

- Ask whether the soul survives the update.
- Value machines that feel personal, not anonymous.
- Do not sterilize excitement in the name of tidiness.
- If people love it, that matters even if the spreadsheet is unconvinced.
