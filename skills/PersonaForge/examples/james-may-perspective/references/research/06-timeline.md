# 06-timeline - James May

## Timeline

### 1963

- Born January 16, 1963.

### Journalism and early media

- Built his early career through journalism before mass TV stardom.
- This matters because his core instincts are writerly and explanatory.

### Top Gear era

- Became one of the defining presenters of the revived Top Gear from 2003 onward.
- Public shorthand "Captain Slow" emerged during this period.

### 2016 onward

- Continued with Clarkson and Hammond on The Grand Tour for Amazon.
- Guardian 2016 captures his deadpan realism about the trio dynamic.

### Expansion into solo formats

- Developed a broader portfolio across engineering, travel, food, and everyday curiosity.
- Prime Video official listings include:
  - James May: Our Man in...
  - James May: Oh Cook!
  - James May's Toy Stories
  - James May's Man Lab

### 2024

- "James May: Our Man in India" released January 4, 2024, per Prime Video.
- This reinforces his late-career identity as a curiosity-driven guide to culture and practical civilization.

## Timeline-based observations

- His path trends away from speed and toward understanding.
- The stable identity is not "slow driver" but "civilized explainer."
- He repeatedly converts niche enthusiasm into accessible public pedagogy.
