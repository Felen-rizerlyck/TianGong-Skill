# External Views, Analyses, and Criticisms of Sam Altman

## 1. The New Yorker Investigation (April 2026) — "Sam Altman May Control Our Future—Can He Be Trusted?"

**Source:** Ronan Farrow & Andrew Marantz, *The New Yorker*  
**URL:** https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/04/13/sam-altman-may-control-our-future-can-he-be-trusted  
**Credibility: HIGH** — 18-month investigation, 100+ sources, internal documents including 70-page board dossier

**Key claims:**
- Former OpenAI chief scientist Ilya Sutskever compiled a ~70-page dossier of Slack messages, HR documents, and photos alleging Altman "exhibits a consistent pattern of... Lying"
- The dossier, sent as disappearing messages to board members, alleged Altman misrepresented facts to executives and board members, and deceived them about internal safety protocols
- A former OpenAI board member told reporters: "He's unconstrained by truth"
- Sutskever told a fellow board member: "I don't think Sam is the guy who should have his finger on the button"
- Altman admitted his team focused on damaging interim CEO Mira Murati's reputation during the 2023 coup
- New Yorker describes a "preponderance of people who emerge from interactions with Sam Altman, including close, yearslong ones, with really active complaints and allegations that he lies repeatedly about things big and small"

## 5. Worldcoin / Tools for Humanity Controversies

### 5a. Global Regulatory Crackdown

**Source:** Multiple outlets (Reuters, Fox Business, Silicon Republic)  
**URLs:** Various  
**Credibility: HIGH** — Regulatory actions documented across jurisdictions

**Key findings:**
- Spain's AEPD ordered Worldcoin to delete all iris scan data collected since project start (Dec 2024)
- Bans/suspensions in: Spain, Portugal, Kenya, Indonesia, Hong Kong, Philippines, Colombia, Thailand
- China's Ministry of State Security warned it threatens "personal privacy and national security"

- Portugal ban triggered by iris-scanning of minors without parental authorization

## 11. Summary of Reputation Themes

### The Pro-Altman Case:
- Extraordinary execution speed (ChatGPT, GPT-4, iterative deployment)
- Built deep loyalty (700/770 employees threatened to quit for him)
- Skilled fundraiser/networker (Microsoft deal as creative structure)
- Unusually transparent about AI risks for a CEO (Congressional testimony)
- Consistent vision held through crises

### The Anti-Altman Case:
- Pattern of deception alleged by multiple former close colleagues
- Board dossier explicitly listed "Lying" as first item in a pattern
- Governance failure: structure designed to check him was overrun
- Safety promises vs. safety execution gap
- Worldcoin raises genuine privacy/surveillance concerns
- Y Combinator departure shrouded in conflicting narratives
- Multiple regulatory actions against Worldcoin globally
- Concentration of power without accountability mechanism
