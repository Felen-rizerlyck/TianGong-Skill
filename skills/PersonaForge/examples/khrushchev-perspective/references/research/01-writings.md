# Khrushchev Writings & Core Beliefs

## Primary Sources

### Secret Speech (February 25, 1956)
- Full title: "On the Cult of Personality and Its Consequences"
- Delivered at closed 20th Party Congress session
- Key themes:
  - Condemnation of Stalin's "cult of personality"
  - References to Lenin's Testament warning about Stalin
  - Focus on Great Purge victims, military failures pre-1941
  - **Khrushchev's real purpose**: consolidate power, discredit rivals
- **What he DIDN'T criticize**: collectivization, pre-1934 political battles, his own role in purges

### "We Will Bury You" Speech (November 18, 1956)
- At Polish embassy reception
- Original meaning: ideological prediction (communism will outlast capitalism)
- Paraphrasing Marx: "proletariat is the grave-digger of capitalism"
- Later clarification: "Your own working class will bury you"
- Western mistranslation created lasting Cold War impact

### Memoirs (1970, published posthumously)
- "Khrushchev Remembers"
- Composed 1966-1970, smuggled to West via son Sergei
- Self-justifying narrative
- Two Ukrainian ghostwriters prepared most material

## Repeated Core Themes (Appearing ≥3 times = Genuine Belief)

1. **Peaceful Coexistence**
   - "We will outlast you" ideology vs military confrontation
   - But meant ideological struggle continues
   - Evidence: Geneva 1955, Vienna 1961 summits

2. **Agrarian Prosperity as Socialist Goal**
   - "Socialism means first of all full stomachs, felt boots and sheepskin jackets"
   - Virgin Lands Campaign evidence
   - Personal peasant background

3. **Reform Within System**
   - De-Stalinization without systemic change
   - Criticized Stalin's method, not system
   - Maintained one-party rule

4. **Collective Leadership**
   - Championed it rhetorically while ruling autocratically
   - Used to defeat Anti-Party Group
   - His own removal was "most democratic coup"

## Self-Created Terms/Concepts

- **"Cult of personality"** - central to Secret Speech
- **"Peaceful coexistence"** - core foreign policy doctrine
- **"Virgin Lands"** (Tselina) - agricultural campaign
- **"New wave"** - space race terminology
